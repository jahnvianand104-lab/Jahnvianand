About · plain cotton

Cut from more than one cloth.

I have never understood the need to confine myself to one interest. Over the years my curiosity has pulled me in directions I did not plan, and they have started to connect.

I joined Rishi Valley School in the fourth grade. The school follows the philosophy of J. Krishnamurti, with less weight on examinations and rankings and more on questioning, observing and learning for yourself. That freedom let me follow interests that would otherwise have stayed unrelated.

I study Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies and Environmental Science. Accountancy and Environmental Science are my favourites, which sounds like an odd pair until you try to build a business that is good for the planet.

I want to be an entrepreneur who creates employment and opportunity, and leaves the world around the business better off.

Field study · dyeing

Why did one vat give me so many blues?

  1. Grew indigo plants from scratch
  2. Built my own natural dye vat
  3. Dyed fabric and watched the shade shift
  4. Worked out why it kept changing

This was my Environmental Science coursework, and it was far less predictable than I expected. It left me with real respect for the patience and knowledge behind traditional textile practice, and a sharper sense of what the industry costs the environment.

3 of 7 swatches dyed. Each dip goes a shade deeper.
